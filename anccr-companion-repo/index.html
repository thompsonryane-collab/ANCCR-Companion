You already know how to use this.

The hub mirrors the Tableau views and the reconciliation steps PW6 and the FECs use now. Learning it is mostly recognising where things moved, not learning new ideas.

Why it is worth the half hour

The platform does not replace judgement; it removes the hours of lookup that come before judgement. A reconciliation that takes an afternoon in spreadsheets — pull the bill, find the tariff, re-rate it, compare, document — is one tap with the audit trail written for you.

It also protects you. Every figure carries its source and formula, so when a number is challenged you can show the derivation instead of re-doing the work.

What it will not do

  • It will not act on a bill. It flags, explains and drafts; a person disputes.
  • It will not reach beyond approved sources. The list of allowed references (PUCs, vendor systems, EIA, S&P, NOAA) is visible in Settings.
  • It will not hide its reasoning. If a score or forecast cannot be explained, it is not shown.
